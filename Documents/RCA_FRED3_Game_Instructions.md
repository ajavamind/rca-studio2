# Games for Fred III

## Split-Second

Each time a pattern is shown, the first player to match all the flashing spots at the same time scores. The first player to make a score of 100 wins.

---

## Chase

For a two-player game, press `*` for player B, and for a single-player game, press `*` for player A.

Space ship for player A starts at the left, and that for player B (with a hole in the symbol) starts at the right. Both vessels can be moved in the directions of the arrows for each player. A player scores when his vessel captures the opponent's vessel. The first player to reach a score of 50 wins the game.

---

## Mines

For a two-player game, press `*` for player B, and for a single-player game, press `*` for player A.

Space ship for player A starts at the left, and that for player B (with a hole in the symbol) starts at the right. A player scores when the opponent's vessel is entrapped by mines or the other vessel. The first player to reach a score of 50 wins the game.

Mines can be laid by both vessels when either player presses his `*` button.

**Note:** Speed of the game is controlled by the position of the first two switches under the body of the game:

| Speed | Switch 1 | Switch 2 | Switch 3 | Switch 4 |
|-------|----------|----------|----------|----------|
| Fast  | OFF      | OFF      | –        | –        |
| Fast  | ON       | OFF      | –        | –        |
| Fast  | OFF      | ON       | –        | –        |
| Slow  | ON       | ON       | –        | –        |

## Bowling

For each player, the ball is released by the following buttons:

- **Up** = Hook Up
- **Center** = Straight Ball
- **Down** = Hook Down

Other buttons are not used in this game.

---

## Racing

To see the tote board, press both `*` buttons.

Choose a horse (1–4) with the numbered button, then place a bet by holding down `*` until the amount desired is reached.

Odds given on the tote board are realistic odds that any given horse will win. Losers will lose the bet from cash on hand, and winners will receive (amount bet) × (odds).

---

## Maze / Draw

### Maze

To play Maze, hold both `*` buttons down, press `START`, and then release the `*` buttons. The object is for both players to reach the starting position of the other player first. The end target is shown and obtained by pressing the `*` button.

### Draw

The starting maze used above can be changed or replaced by drawing your own.

**To change the existing maze:** Hold just `*` for player B and press and release `START`. You are no longer in Maze mode. Patterns can be added or removed by pressing `*` while moving with the arrows.

**To erase all existing patterns and create new ones:** Hold just `*` for player A and press and release `START`.

### Maze (with custom pattern)

To play Maze with your new pattern, just press and release `START` (without pressing any other buttons).

**Note:** Speed of Maze is determined by the position of the first two switches under the body of the game:

| Speed | Switch 1 | Switch 2 | Switch 3 | Switch 4 |
|-------|----------|----------|----------|----------|
| Fast  | OFF      | OFF      | –        | –        |
| Fast  | ON       | OFF      | –        | –        |
| Fast  | OFF      | ON       | –        | –        |
| Slow  | ON       | ON       | –        | –        |
